Engineering grounded in research.

Firmware architecture and physical fabrication for constrained edge devices.

I am a Mechatronics Engineer specializing in the physical fabrication and firmware architecture of constrained edge devices and autonomous systems.   1. 01 Preprint

    Development of a Semi-Autonomous Navigation System for a Pipeline Monitoring Robot

    Aminu, M. A. (2026)

    A Raspberry-Pi-based pipeline monitoring robot combining ultrasonic obstacle detection, autonomous halting and RESTful API teleoperation. Reported performance: 95.00% obstacle-detection accuracy, a 1.02 s mean autonomous halt response and 5.15 s IoT email-alert latency.

    View Preprint (PDF)
  2. 02 BUILD26 CONFERENCE

    Hardware-in-the-Loop Benchmarking of Edge-NILM on RISC-V Silicon: Decentralized Demand-Side Management for Resilient African Solar Mini-Grids

    Aminu, M. A. | BUILD26 Conference, Abuja (2026)

    A hardware-in-the-loop benchmark of an integer-quantized 1D-CNN running fully offline on an ESP32-C3 RISC-V microcontroller for non-intrusive load monitoring. Reported results: 2.510 ms inference latency, 5.18 KB Flash / 25.00 KB SRAM footprint and a 99.00% reduction in telemetry bandwidth versus continuous cloud streaming.
